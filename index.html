FAQ

What everyone asks before getting started.

How much does it cost?

We go over it in 20 minutes, after you've seen the demo. That way I quote your case, not a generic package. You get a fixed price, in writing.

Just send me the price here and we'll see.

If I give you a number before you see what's included, we'll be talking about different things. Start with the demo, then we set up the call.

How long until it's up and running?

Usually 14 days. If it's only FAQs, 7. If we add quotes or several services, up to 30. The date is in writing before we start.

I'm not sure it works for my kind of business.

If people keep asking you the same questions and you get appointment or quote requests, it works. In the call I show you the demo with questions from your business, and if it's not for you, I'll tell you.

What if the AI makes things up?

It only uses the information you approve. If it doesn't know something, it doesn't invent: it hands over to a person. And it never confirms appointments or gives diagnoses.

What if it doesn't work the way I expected?

Before I build anything, you get in writing what it does and what it doesn't. After the first week of use we do a round of adjustments with real questions, and I fix whatever's needed.

Will I be stuck with you afterwards?

No. The assistant lives on your website and the information is yours. I leave everything documented. Monthly support is optional and you can cancel it with one month's notice. And you don't need to know about technology to use it.

What happens to my clients' data?

It only asks for what's needed for the request, never medical details, and it tells people they're talking to an AI assistant. I also explain in writing where each piece of data is stored.
